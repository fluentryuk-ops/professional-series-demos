📑 What you'll learn

Interrupting isn't always rude.

In professional meetings, knowing when and how to enter a conversation can help you:

  • contribute before the discussion moves on
  • ask an important clarifying question
  • bring a conversation back on track
  • return to a point when you've been talked over
  • participate more confidently in video calls
  • hand the conversation back smoothly when you've finished

How the phrases are organised

Each section focuses on one real meeting situation. You'll see phrases grouped by tone:

Friendly / Natural
Relaxed team conversations and informal meetings.
Professional / Neutral
Standard workplace communication.
Formal / Direct
More careful, structured or high-stakes situations.

How to get the most from this toolkit

  1. Choose your situation
    Start with the section that matches a situation you regularly experience.
  2. Learn the pattern, not just the phrase
    Notice how professionals signal, interrupt, make their point and return the floor.
  3. Practise out loud
    Use the audio to hear the rhythm and pronunciation.
  4. Use one phrase in a real meeting
    Don't try to memorise everything. Choose one or two phrases and start using them.
